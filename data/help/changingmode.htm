Changing mode in the Longman Writing Assistant

On the original CD-ROM the Writing Assistant could shrink into a small window that sat on the desktop while you worked elsewhere, and switch back to the full dictionary with 'Writing Assistant' and 'Go to Dictionary' buttons.

This Mac app has no window modes. Everything lives in its three menus: look words up in the Dictionary, study vocabulary by topic in Exams Coach, and read the guides here. The Assistant's content travels with each entry instead — synonyms in Similar words, collocations in Phrases, and typical slips in Common mistakes.
