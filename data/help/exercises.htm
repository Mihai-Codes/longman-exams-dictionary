Exercises

The original CD-ROM ran interactive exercises in Practice or Test Mode, with hints, scoring and a timer.

This app has no exercises. Study vocabulary by topic in Exams Coach, and read the exam guides (FCE, CAE, IELTS, TOEIC, TOEFL) in this Guide. On each dictionary entry, Similar words, Phrases and Common mistakes cover choosing a precise word.
