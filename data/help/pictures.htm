Pictures

Note: in this Mac app every illustrated entry carries an Image button that opens its picture large. Picture labels are not clickable definitions. What follows describes the original CD-ROM.

The dictionary contains many pictures to help make entries even clearer.

Clicking on the pictures opens them in a larger window to make them easier to see and study.

If the picture has labels, click on them to get a quick definition. This makes it easy to learn the names for parts of things.

Use the multimedia search to find a list of all the pictures in the CD-ROM.
