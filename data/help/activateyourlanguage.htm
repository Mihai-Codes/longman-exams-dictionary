Activate your language

The original CD-ROM ‘Activate your language’ box suggested more precise words for common meaning areas — for example that heat can describe a room, a place, or the weather, and that sweltering fits climate while stifling fits a room.

This app has no Activate your language box and no yellow or red arrows into an Activator section. Look the word up in Dictionary and read Similar words, or browse related topic words in Exams Coach. See How the Activator is organized.
