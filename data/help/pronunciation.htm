Pronunciation of words

You can listen to every headword. Open an entry and click Audio beside the headword. If a recovered recording exists it plays; otherwise the Mac speaks the word.

The phonetic transcription sits in a badge under the headword, so you can read the sounds as well as hear them. Audio plays on demand — it waits until you ask, so nothing sounds on its own when an entry opens.

To hear a word you are not sure how to spell, search a close spelling and pick from the list — see dictionary search.
