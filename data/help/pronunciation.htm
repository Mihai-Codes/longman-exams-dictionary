Pronunciation of words

You can listen to every headword. Open an entry and click Audio beside the headword. If a recovered recording exists it plays; otherwise the Mac speaks the word.

Pronunciation symbols appear in a badge under the headword. There is no settings menu to hide them, no British/American switch, and no recorder to compare your own voice. Audio is on demand — it does not play automatically when the entry opens.

There is no pronunciation search. Type a spelling in Dictionary instead. See pronunciation search.
