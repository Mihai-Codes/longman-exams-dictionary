Web/email Pop Up Mode

On the CD-ROM a shrunken dictionary window could hover over other programs so you could look words up while reading mail or the web.

There is no pop-up window in this Mac app — and nothing that needs one. Press ⌘K anywhere for search, or open the Dictionary menu and type: results appear as you type, and any word you open keeps its full entry, examples and illustrations.
