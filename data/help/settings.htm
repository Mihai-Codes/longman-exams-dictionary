Settings

The CD-ROM kept look-and-display controls — text size, British or American pronunciation, syllable dots, phonetic symbols — behind a tools-button settings menu.

This Mac app keeps the handful of controls that matter where you can see them: the Motion button in the toolbar plays effects in full or follows your Mac's Reduce Motion setting, and the Audio button on every entry reads the headword aloud. Light and dark appearance follow your Mac's System Settings, and entry text size is fixed for comfortable reading.
