How the Activator is organized

Unlike ordinary dictionaries, which you use to look up words you do not know, the Longman Language Activator starts with words you do know and takes you to more precise words and phrases.

On the original CD-ROM those words were grouped under keywords such as HAPPY (glad, pleased, delighted). This app has no Activator index. Topic groups live in Exams Coach; on each dictionary entry, Similar words, Phrases and Common mistakes cover choosing a precise word.
