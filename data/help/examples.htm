Examples

The best way to understand English is to see how it is used. Examples sit right beside the meaning they illustrate, on the same entry.

A typical entry shows example sentences under Definition, then corpus lines from books and newspapers with the source named under each one.

Phrases on the same page lists the word's common combinations. Scroll the entry; everything is already there.

To keep an example, select its text and copy it with ⌘C. See copy and paste.
