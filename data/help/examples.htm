Examples

Note: in this Mac app examples sit with their entry, with the source named under each one, and Phrases lists the word's common combinations. What follows describes the original CD-ROM banks.

The best way to understand English is to see how it is used. There are many examples within the text. This CD-ROM shows thousands of word combinations and over a million sentence examples!

· 80,000 examples from other Longman dictionaries.

· Over a million sentences from books, magazines, etc.

The origin of the example is indicated in the examples bank window.

The examples bank works in conjunction with the phrase bank. Click on a phrase in the phrase bank and examples containing the phrase will appear in the examples bank.

The phrase bank also shows words commonly used with the word you are studying. Click on any of these and examples will be shown in the examples bank window.

You can print or copy and paste any of the examples into a word processor.
