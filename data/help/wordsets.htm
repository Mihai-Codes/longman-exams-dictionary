Word sets

Many words in the dictionary are grouped into word sets — collections of words that belong to the same subject area. The word 'trunk', for example, has several different meanings and occurs in several word sets.

In this app, word sets live in Exams Coach: open the Coach menu and browse or search the topics — each topic lists the words examiners expect around it, and tapping any word opens its full dictionary entry.

Individual entries also show Similar words — related words from the same area of meaning, with a short gloss for each.
