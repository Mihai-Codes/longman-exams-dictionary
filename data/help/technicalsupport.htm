Technical support

This is an unofficial Mac port of the 2006 Longman Exams Dictionary, not Pearson support. Pearson’s original CD-ROM support pages are no longer the place to get help with this app.

For issues with this Mac app, open an issue on the project at https://github.com/Mihai-Codes/longman-exams-dictionary

The original Pearson dictionary site was http://www.longman.com/dictionaries/
