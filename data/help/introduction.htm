Searching for phrases

Look up over 207,000 words, phrases, and meanings in the Longman Exams Dictionary. If the word(s) you type are in the dictionary, they will appear in the index list that will drop down as you type. Click OK or on a word in the list to go directly to the entry.

Spellcheck

If you spell a word incorrectly, the dictionary lists words which are close to the word you typed. Click on the one you want. You can turn this function on and off in the tools menu.

Selecting entries

If there are several entries that contain your choice of word you will be presented with a list. Open and close entries using the arrow buttons.

Information about an entry

The blue toolbar at the side of the entry shows what extra information is included for the word throw (verb)..

In this example above you can:

  • listen to the pronunciation of the word
  • see a menu to help you navigate around this large entry
  • look at the thesaurus for throw
  • see common errors
  • see the word origin of the verb throw
  • see all verb forms
  • see which word sets are associated with the verb throw
  • write your own user note.

 

There are some other buttons that will help you move around in the dictionary. Use the back arrow to see the last entry you looked at, and use the forward arrow to move forward in a list of previously viewed entries.

Secondary definitions

If you do not understand a word in the definition, double-click on that word to see its definition in a new window. All definitions are written using the 2000-word Longman Defining Vocabulary.

If you want to see the whole entry, click on the 'Go to entry' button.

Phrases

When you look up a word in the dictionary, pharses using that word will be listed in the phrase bank and featured in the examples bank.
