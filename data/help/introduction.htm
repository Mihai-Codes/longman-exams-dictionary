Searching for phrases

Look up over 207,000 words, phrases, and meanings in the Longman Exams Dictionary. As you type in the Dictionary search box, matching headwords appear in the list, and the first match completes ahead of your typing. Tap a word in the list, or press Tab to accept the completion, to open its entry.

Spellcheck

If you spell a word incorrectly, the search still lists close matches — the dictionary searches pronunciation-tolerant and partial matches automatically. Tap the one you want.

Selecting entries

If a word has several entries (for example, as a noun and a verb), all of them appear in the results list. Tap any of them to open it, and use the A–Z letters beside the list to page through the whole dictionary alphabetically.

Information about an entry

Every entry shows its full information directly on the page — no toolbars or hidden panels. A typical entry includes:

  • its definition, senses, and examples
  • spoken and written frequency bands (word frequency)
  • verb forms for verbs
  • common mistakes learners make with the word
  • phrases and collocations
  • similar words and word families
  • corpus examples from books and newspapers

Three buttons sit beside the headword: Save keeps the word in your saved list, Audio plays the pronunciation aloud, and Image opens the entry's illustration when one exists. The streak counter tracks how many days in a row you have looked words up.

Secondary definitions

All definitions are written using the 2000-word Longman Defining Vocabulary, so they stay understandable. If a word inside a definition is unfamiliar, type it in the search box to open its own entry — cross-references in Common mistakes are tappable and jump straight to the related entry.

Phrases

When you look up a word in the dictionary, phrases using that word are listed in the phrase bank and featured in the examples bank.
