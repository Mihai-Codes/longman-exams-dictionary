Search

Type in the Dictionary search box, or press ⌘K from anywhere. Matching headwords appear as you type; press Tab to accept the completion, or tap a word in the list to open it.

Each kind of lookup has its own place:

Dictionary search — words, phrases and meanings

Pictures — on illustrated entries, with an Image button

Word sets — subject groups in Exams Coach

Pronunciation — the Audio button beside every headword

Word frequency — S and W badges beside the headword
