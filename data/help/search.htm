Search

There is no separate Search button. Type in the Dictionary search box, or press ⌘K from anywhere. Matching headwords appear as you type; press Tab to accept the completion, or tap a word in the list to open it.

The original CD-ROM kept extra searches behind a Search menu. This is where those jobs went:

Dictionary search — looking up words and phrases

Multimedia search — pictures live on illustrated entries

Subject search — subject groups live in Exams Coach

Word origin search — not in this app

Pronunciation search — not in this app; use Audio on the entry

Word frequency — S and W badges beside the headword
