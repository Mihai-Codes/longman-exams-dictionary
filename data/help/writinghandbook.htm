Writing Handbook

The original CD-ROM Writing Handbook had an Essay Activator (language for exam essay answers), a Topic Activator (language for important topics), and writing exercises you compared with a sample answer on paper.

This app has no Essay Activator, Topic Activator screens, or writing exercises. Topic vocabulary lives in Exams Coach. On each dictionary entry, Similar words, Phrases and Common mistakes cover choosing a precise word. See How the Activator is organized.
