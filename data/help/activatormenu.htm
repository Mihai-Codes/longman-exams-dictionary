Choosing a precise word

When you want a better word than very happy or a more exact verb than clean, start from the word you already have and open its entry. Three cards on that page do the work:

Similar words — related words from the same area of meaning, each with a short gloss, so happy leads you to overjoyed, delighted and content.

Phrases — the combinations that sound natural, such as magic circle and magic moment.

Common mistakes — a wrong form, a right form, and a short note explaining the difference.

For vocabulary grouped by subject rather than by one word, browse the 762 topics in Exams Coach. Each topic lists the words examiners expect around it, and tapping any word opens its entry.
