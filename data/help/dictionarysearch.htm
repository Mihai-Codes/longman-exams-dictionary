Dictionary search

Type in the Dictionary search box to locate an entry. Matching headwords appear as you type. Tap a word, or press Tab to accept the completion, to open it.

Search tries exact headwords first, then prefixes, then inflected forms (so went finds go), then full-text matches in definitions. Close spellings still list nearby words — there is no separate spellcheck command.

You do not need a wildcard. Prefix matching is automatic, so typing lia lists words that begin with those letters. There are no part-of-speech or style filters, and no separate window of results: the list is the Dictionary pane.

Use the A–Z letters beside the list to page through the whole dictionary alphabetically. Frequency is not a search filter — it appears as S and W badges on the entry.

See the Grammar Handbook note for parts of speech; this app has no grammar exercises.
