Dictionary search

Type in the Dictionary search box to locate an entry. Matching headwords appear as you type. Tap a word, or press Tab to accept the completion, to open it.

Search tries exact headwords first, then prefixes, then inflected forms (so went finds go), then full-text matches in definitions. Close spellings still list nearby words, so a mistyped word reaches the right entry.

Prefix matching is automatic, so typing lia lists words that begin with those letters — no wildcards needed. Results stay in the Dictionary pane as you type.

Use the A–Z letters beside the list to page through the whole dictionary alphabetically. Frequency appears as S and W badges on the entry, and every section of an entry sits on the page itself — see what an entry shows.
